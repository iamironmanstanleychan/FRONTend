The once-in-a-lifetime list

The world, a little more wonderful.

Six distinct corners of the world, each with its own rhythm, route and reason to go.

01 / ISLAND WILDERNESS Yemen · Arabian Sea

Socotra Island

A living, breathing world apart.

Signature

See the Dragon Blood Trees on the Dixam Plateau, then camp beneath Socotra’s exceptionally clear night sky.

How to go

Arrange a licensed local expedition first; access is commonly by limited charter from Abu Dhabi, with permits and schedules subject to change.

Best time

October–April

Budget

US$200–350 / day
Guided trip; flights extra

02 / SKY & STONE Türkiye · Central Anatolia

Cappadocia

A sunrise worth setting an alarm for.

Signature

Watch hot-air balloons rise over the fairy chimneys at first light; explore the valleys on foot after breakfast.

How to go

Fly to Nevşehir or Kayseri via Istanbul, then take a shuttle or transfer to Göreme and the surrounding towns.

Best time

April–June
September–October

Budget

US$90–180 / day
Balloon flight extra

03 / ANCIENT KINGDOM Myanmar · Mandalay Region

Bagan

A sea of temples beneath a wide-open sky.

Signature

Wander among the brick pagodas at dawn, and take in the temple plain from an approved viewpoint at sunset.

How to go

When travel is safe and permitted, fly to Nyaung U from Yangon or Mandalay, or take a domestic bus; check current local access before planning.

Best time

November–February

Budget

US$45–100 / day
Excluding international flights

04 / WATER & FOREST Croatia · Lika-Senj County

Plitvice Lakes

Waterfalls woven through an emerald forest.

Signature

Follow the wooden boardwalks around the lakes and waterfalls, with an early start to enjoy the trails before the crowds.

How to go

Join a bus or small-group tour from Zagreb, Zadar or Split; reserve park entry and check seasonal shuttle and boat times.

Best time

May–June
September–October

Budget

US$100–200 / day
Park ticket varies by season

05 / DESERT HERITAGE Saudi Arabia · AlUla

AlUla & Hegra

Ancient stories carved into sandstone.

Signature

Tour Hegra’s Nabataean tombs, then watch the last light spill across Elephant Rock and AlUla’s sandstone canyons.

How to go

Fly to AlUla International Airport from Riyadh or Jeddah when flights operate; reserve timed Hegra tours and local transport ahead.

Best time

October–March

Budget

US$180–350 / day
Tours and stays vary

Watch on YouTube ↗
06 / WILD FRONTIER Madagascar · Melaky Region

Tsingy de Bemaraha

A forest of stone at the edge of the map.

Signature

Climb the limestone pinnacles and cross suspension bridges with a local guide in this UNESCO-listed karst landscape.

How to go

Travel overland from Antananarivo to the west coast, then continue by 4×4 and ferry to Bekopaka; use a local guide in the park.

Best time

May–October

Budget

US$120–250 / day
Guides and 4×4 add cost
